Privacy policy

What we collect, why, and what you can ask us

Who we are

Pandas Lab, run by Michele Barison, is the data controller. Contact: info@pandaslab.it.

What we collect

Only what you give us in the case-file form: your email address, the case you asked for and the language of the page. We do not ask for anything else.

Why

To send you the case pack and, since you agreed, emails about new cases. The legal basis is your consent (GDPR art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email, or by writing to us.

Who handles it

The list is kept on MailerLite (UAB MailerLite, Vilnius, Lithuania, EU), which acts as our processor. We do not sell or share your email with anyone else.

How long

Until you unsubscribe. After that your address is removed from the active list.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to its use, or to receive it in a portable format, by writing to info@pandaslab.it. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

Visiting the site

The site counts visits with GoatCounter, which sets no cookies and stores no personal data. Videos are embedded through youtube-nocookie.com; fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address to deliver them.
