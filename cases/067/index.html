What if gravity doubled tonight?

You wouldn't be crushed. You'd be stuck. Here is what doubling Earth's pull would do to your body, to the trees, to the sky and to the way off the planet.

01 · The Question

Twice the pull, overnight

Standard gravity is 9.80665 m/s² OBSERVED. Weight is mass times gravity, so if gravity doubled, you would weigh twice as much SCENARIO.

Every staircase would feel like climbing with a second you on your back. The real question is not whether you survive the first night. It's how long a body can live like that.

02 · The Evidence

Four exhibits

ESTIMATE
22 → 44 mmHg

To push blood up to your brain, your heart works against about 22 mmHg at 1 g. At 2 g, that column of blood weighs twice as much: about 44 mmHg ESTIMATE.

C3 · Journal of Military and Veterans' Health (JMVH) · jmvh.org
OBSERVED
~4.5–5 g

Relaxed, people tolerate about 4.5–5 g head-to-feet before blacking out OBSERVED, so 2 g alone won't knock you out. But no human has ever lived at 2 g for long OBSERVED.

C4 · Journal of Military and Veterans' Health (JMVH) · jmvh.org
HYPOTHESIS
~122–130 m

Lifting water to the top limits how tall trees can grow: about 122–130 m (400–427 ft), according to a study of the tallest redwoods HYPOTHESIS. With water twice as heavy, that ceiling would drop SCENARIO.

C7 · Koch, Sillett, Jennings et al. 2004 · The limits to tree height, Nature 428:851 · doi.org
SCENARIO
+41%

If gravity doubled because Earth had twice the mass at the same size, escape velocity would rise from 11.2 km/s to 15.8 km/s SCENARIO.

C9 · NASA NSSDC · Earth Fact Sheet · nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov

03 · The Simulation

Steve's test

We tried it on Steve, our felt puppet, in a physics model: same muscles, then the weight doubles. Standing at 1 g, he went down 3.7 s after gravity switched to 2 g SIMULATION.

It's a toy model of a puppet, not of a human body. It shows the idea, same strength against double the load, not how long a person would last.

And the sky? The height over which air thins out would drop from 8.5 km to about 4.25 km if the air kept the same temperature SCENARIO.

04 · The Verdict

Not crushed. Kept.

What we know: your weight would double, your heart would push against about twice the pressure, and leaving the planet would take about 41% more speed.

What we don't: how a human body copes with 2 g for months or years. Nobody has tried OBSERVED. Gravity wouldn't crush you. It would keep you.
